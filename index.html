About me

Research Scientist · Nanyang Technological University

I am a Research Scientist at Nanyang Technological University, supervised by Prof. Yonggang Wen. I was a postdoctoral fellow at Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Computing from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University in January 2026, supervised by Prof. Dan Wang. I also hold a master's degree from the Polytechnic University of Turin and a bachelor's degree from Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research lies at the intersection of sustainable computing, carbon-aware computing, and AI for energy and sustainability. Before my PhD, I worked as an Algorithm Engineer at Huawei and ICBC SDC.
